Why we're building in watersports

← Back to journal

Robridge has a strong passion across watersports and love to see new people getting involved and having fun. We find new and innovative ways in which we can spread enthusiasm for these sports and help people develop their skills.

Whether that's gaining confidence in your own boats or boards or going for gold, we love to see the positive impacts that getting involved in watersports can have.

Why watersports?

The world of watersports can often be seen as an ultra competitive scene, dominated by the elites but in reality its a vast community diversified across all different levels of experience and skill with no pressure at all to be the best. Its such a warm, welcoming community and we want to promote that, whether your getting into a watersport, switching from one to another or trying to change your style from cruising around to going for gold, were here for it!

The community

The community that has been built around watersports has always been a special one, no matter where you are, what fleet your in or what level your at its a warm, welcoming world. Theres always people there to support you whether on the beach or on the water and its a perfect space to seek help and advice whenever you need it.

What we want to change

Robridge isnt here to change the community or try and "fix" anything, were here to BUILD the community and spread inspiration, helping you to get on the water with your new helm or crew or share stories of people who have gone on to achieve it all and show you what your capeable of and where watersports can take you.

The bigger picture

The end goal for Robridge is to build a brand that people can trust and rely on. we want to help people go on to achieve incredible things and provide what they need to be able to conqure their dreams whether that be anything from the olympic to the americas cup, we want to help get you there. So please show your support and help us grow our brand so we can go on to do this!
